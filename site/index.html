Built on the evidence

2026 ACC/AHA multisociety dyslipidemia guideline, the PREVENT equations (Khan et al., Circulation 2024) and outcome trials such as IMPROVE-IT, FOURIER and CLEAR Outcomes.

Shows its reasoning

Each step lists its source and class of recommendation. Where evidence is unclear, the tool flags it for the clinician instead of guessing.

Data stays with you

Calculations run in your browser. Patient information is not sent to or stored on this site.

For clinicians. LDL Goal Navigator is decision support. It does not replace clinical judgment, is not FDA-cleared, and is under validation. Review every recommendation before acting on it.
